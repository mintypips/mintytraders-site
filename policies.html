Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

💳 Monthly Subscription

Your MintyTraders subscription gives you access to automated trading signals, the live dashboard, and ongoing bot updates.

  • Cancel anytime — no lock-in, no cancellation fees. Cancel before your next billing date and you will not be charged again.
  • Access continues until the end of your current billing period after cancellation.
  • No partial refunds for unused days within a billing period that has already been charged.
  • Downgrade to a lower tier takes effect on the next billing cycle.

📌 To cancel, simply email us at support@mintytraders.com or message us on Telegram and we'll process it within 24 hours.

⚙️ One-Time Setup Fee

The setup fee covers account onboarding, API configuration, strategy testing on your account, and direct technical support to get you live.

  • Non-refundable once your accounts have been connected and live trading has begun.
  • Refundable in full if we are unable to successfully connect your account within 7 business days of payment, through no fault of your own.
  • Partial refund may be considered at our discretion if onboarding was not completed due to compatibility issues outside your control.

🎓 Mentorship Program

All mentorship payments are subject to the following terms:

  • Non-refundable once program access has been granted or sessions have begun.
  • Full refund if you cancel within 48 hours of purchase and no sessions or materials have been accessed.
  • Deferral is available — if you need to pause, we can hold your spot for up to 60 days at no charge.
  • No refunds for dissatisfaction with trading results — past performance is not indicative of future results.

⛔ Non-Refundable Situations

The following are not eligible for refunds under any circumstances:

  • Trading losses incurred while using MintyTraders signals or automation
  • Losses resulting from broker-side execution issues, slippage, or platform outages
  • Fees already paid for months where the service was active and accessible
  • Setup fees after your accounts have been successfully onboarded
  • Requests made more than 30 days after the charge date

⚠️ Important: MintyTraders provides trading signals and automation software, not financial advice. We are not responsible for trading losses. Always trade with risk you can afford to lose.

⚖️ Dispute Resolution

If you believe you've been charged in error or have a billing concern, please contact us before filing a chargeback with your bank. We resolve billing disputes quickly and fairly.

  • Contact us within 30 days of the charge for fastest resolution

  • We aim to respond within 1 business day
  • Chargebacks filed without first contacting support may result in account suspension

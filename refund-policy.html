1. Monthly Subscription — Cancellation

You may cancel your MintyTraders subscription at any time with no penalties or cancellation fees.

  • Cancel before your next billing date and you will not be charged again
  • Access continues until the end of your current paid billing period
  • Account data and trade history retained for 90 days after cancellation
  • No minimum commitment period — month-to-month

📌 To cancel, email support@mintytraders.com or message us on Telegram. We process all cancellations within 1 business day and confirm by email.

2. Monthly Subscription — Refunds

Monthly subscription fees are generally non-refundable once a billing period has begun and access has been granted. The following exceptions apply:

48-Hour New Subscriber Window Eligible

  • First-time subscribers may request a full refund within 48 hours of their initial payment, provided no signals have been delivered or acted upon. Granted at our discretion.

Service Outage Credit Eligible

  • If our service experiences a documented outage of more than 72 continuous hours, you may request a prorated credit for the affected period.

Standard Monthly Charges Not Eligible

  • Refunds are not issued for billing periods where service was accessible and active, regardless of whether you personally used the service
  • Refunds are not issued because signals resulted in trading losses
  • Requests made more than 30 days after the charge are not eligible

3. Setup & Onboarding Fee

The one-time setup fee covers technical onboarding, account configuration, broker API integration, and initial support.

Non-Refundable After Onboarding

  • Once your accounts have been successfully connected and live trading has begun, the setup fee is non-refundable

Full Refund Eligible

  • If we are unable to successfully onboard your account within 7 business days of payment, through no fault of your own, you are entitled to a full refund of the setup fee

Partial Refund Case by Case

  • May be considered at our discretion for incomplete onboarding due to verified broker compatibility issues outside your control

4. Mentorship Program

Full Refund Eligible

  • Cancel within 48 hours of purchase and no sessions or digital materials have been accessed

Non-Refundable After Access

  • Once program access has been granted or any session has taken place, no refund will be issued
  • Refunds are not issued for dissatisfaction with trading outcomes or results

Deferral Option Available

  • Your enrollment can be paused for up to 60 days at no charge upon written request

5. Non-Refundable Situations

The following are never eligible for refunds under any circumstances:

  • Trading losses incurred while using MintyTraders signals or automation
  • Losses resulting from broker-side execution issues, slippage, or platform outages
  • Fees already paid for months where the service was active and accessible
  • Setup fees after your accounts have been successfully onboarded
  • Refund requests submitted more than 30 days after the charge date
  • Dissatisfaction with trading results or signal accuracy

⚠️ MintyTraders provides trading signals and automation software, not financial advice. We are not responsible for trading losses. Always trade with capital you can afford to lose.

6. Dispute & Chargeback Process

If you believe you've been charged in error, please contact us before filing a chargeback with your bank or payment provider. We resolve billing disputes quickly and fairly.

  • Contact us within 30 days of the charge for fastest resolution
  • Provide your account email and a clear description of the issue
  • We aim to respond within 1 business day

Filing a chargeback without first contacting our support team may result in immediate account suspension and may be considered a breach of our Terms of Service. We take fraudulent chargebacks seriously and will contest them with full documentation.

7. How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund request:

  • Email support@mintytraders.com with subject line: "Refund Request — [Your Email]"
  • Include your account email, the charge date, amount, and reason for the request
  • We will respond within 1 business day
  • Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days depending on your payment method
